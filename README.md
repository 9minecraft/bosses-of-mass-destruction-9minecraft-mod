# Bosses of Mass Destruction — Challenge and Conquer Deadly Bosses

Bosses of Mass Destruction 9minecraft Mod introduces four unique boss encounters across your world. Each encounter requires a specific summoning ritual and structure, giving you full control over when battles begin. You face custom animated monsters with clear attack patterns, dodgeable moves, and dedicated boss health bars. Defeating these foes rewards special crafting items like ancient anima and void thorns to forge powerful gear.

Adds four custom boss encounters across the Overworld, Nether, and End.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Barribob**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
